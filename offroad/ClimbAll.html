ClimbAll

A gravel pit with wrecked cars lying around  •  drive over them to the far end

Arrow keys or WASD: up = throttle, down = brake / reverse, left and right = steer, Space = handbrake

Gears: 1 = Crawl (most climbing power, slowest)  •  2 = Low  •  3 = High  •  shift with keys 1 2 3 or Q (down) / E (up)  •  low gears also hold you back going downhill

Four independent suspensions: each wheel rises and falls on its own to keep the truck level, so you can drive one side up over a car while the other side stays on the ground

Go slow and pick your line  •  too much lean, too much throttle on a steep climb or hard braking downhill will tip you over

C = switch cockpit / behind view  •  R = right the truck if flipped, or back to last flag (time penalty)  •  T = restart  •  M = sound

Rival trucks:   each rival starts 10 seconds after the one before
