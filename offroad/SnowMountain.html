Snow Mountain

An Arctic 6x6 snow machine on open snow mountains and steep hills with bumps and holes  •  drive anywhere, just for fun

Arrow keys or WASD: up = throttle, down = brake / reverse, left and right = steer, Space = handbrake

Gears: 1 = Crawl (most climbing power, slowest)  •  2 = Low  •  3 = High  •  shift with keys 1 2 3 or Q (down) / E (up)  •  low gears also hold you back going downhill

Normal suspension: the machine leans and rocks with the terrain, so pick your line around the deepest holes and ice

Go anywhere you like — it climbs just about anything

C = switch cockpit / behind view  •  R = right the machine if flipped, or back to the start  •  T = restart  •  M = sound
